TRACK 02 — AI RISK MANAGER
Goal: stop the merchant losing money to fraud, returns and 
chargebacks.
Build: a working detector, verifier or auto-responder for ONE class

Why it matters now: AI-enabled fraud is hitting Indian BFSI. Returns 
and chargebacks quietly eat margin.
Example ideas they list: chargeback evidence responder, return-risk

THE BAR: honest metrics, including the cost of false positives. 
Strictly defense-only — anything that could be used for offense is 
disqualified.

TRACK 03 — AI REVENUE RECOVERY
Goal: find revenue slipping away and win it back.
Build: an agent that detects revenue at risk, decides the right 
intervention, and runs a bounded recovery workflow. Covers payment 
failures, checkout abandonment, and overdue receivables.
Why it matters now: revenue loss happens in slow steps — a payment 
degrades, a checkout is abandoned, a subscription fails, an invoice 
goes overdue. AI can now close the loop from detecting to diagnosing 
to choosing an intervention to recovering the money.
Example ideas they list: payment degradation to root cause to 
recovery action, checkout drop-off recovery, failed-subscription 
recovery, B2B receivables chaser, mandate retry sequencer, Hinglish 
voice recovery, promise-to-pay tracker.
THE BAR: don't just find the problem. Show measured money recovered 
across a batch, with compliant escalation, stopping rules, and an 
audit trail.

TRACK 04 — AI FINANCE CONTROLLER
Goal: run the books and the cash position.
Build: an agent that closes one finance-ops loop across a batch of

Example ideas they list: multi-source reconciliation, settlement Q&A 
agent, forward cash forecaster, tax-line matcher.
THE BAR: throughput plus measured accuracy plus an honest exception 
list. One cherry-picked match proves nothing.
